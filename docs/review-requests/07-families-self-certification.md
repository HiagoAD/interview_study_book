# The Families policy sentence says Google Play certifies the ads SDKs

**Kind:** Accuracy · **Priority:** Low · **Touches:** prose (one clause)

## Location

- `content/mobile-platform/07-sdk-integration.md:316`: “[Google Play's Families policy] allows ads to children, or to users of unknown age, only through ads SDKs it has self-certified for families”

## Evidence

The antecedent of “it” is Google Play, but in this program the SDK providers certify themselves and Google Play publishes the list. The policy page (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335?hl=en, fetched 2026-09-29) says: “If your app displays ads to children or to users of unknown age, you must: Only use Google Play Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs to display ads to those users”, and “you must use only Families self-certified ads SDK versions … refer here to see the current list of Families Self-Certified ads SDK versions.” As written, the sentence has Google vetting SDKs that in fact vouch for themselves.

## Proposed fix

“… allows ads to children, or to users of unknown age, only through ads SDK versions on its list of Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs, and it bars …”
